# Sales Tracker & Dashboard (Excel)

A simple Excel sales tracker and monthly dashboard that shows a small retail shop its revenue, profit, best sellers, and daily sales trends, using only Microsoft Excel.

![Sales Dashboard](sales-dashboard.png)

## Project Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Industry** | Retail (small shop) |
| **Tool** | Microsoft Excel |
| **Dataset** | Simulated daily sales records for October 2026 |
| **Categories** | Groceries, Household, Stationery, Personal Care |

## Business Problem

A small retail business records its sales but has no quick way to see how it is performing. The owner can't easily tell the month's total revenue and profit, which products and categories earn the most, or how sales change from day to day.

## What I Built

- **Sales Tracker:** a data entry sheet where each sale is recorded with date, product, category, quantity, unit price, and unit cost. Revenue, total cost, and profit are calculated automatically for each row.
- **Sales Dashboard:** a one-page summary with:
  - A month selector (dropdown)
  - Summary cards: Total Sales, Highest Sale, Total Profit
  - Total Revenue by Category chart
  - Daily Revenue Trend chart
  - A transaction table that updates with the selected month

## Key Findings (October 2026)

- **Total sales were KSh 305,050** and **total profit was KSh 67,786**, a profit margin of about **22%**.
- **Groceries generated roughly 60% of revenue**, well ahead of Household, Stationery, and Personal Care.
- **Cooking Oil produced the largest single sale** at KSh 8,750.
- **Margins vary a lot by product.** Staples such as Cooking Oil, Maize Flour, and Sugar earn roughly 14% to 21%, while Notebooks and Pen Packs earn roughly 33% to 44%.
- **Daily revenue was uneven**, from about KSh 2,000 on the slowest days to about KSh 29,000 on the peak day (around 25 October).
